`dreamscale sim robolab` runs RoboLab's Franka tabletop tasks on our simulators
and drives them with a model in the cloud. Nothing installs on your computer.

## What you need

- The latest CLI: `uv tool upgrade dreamscale`.
- `dreamscale login`, and credits on your account.

## Run a task

```bash
dreamscale sim robolab --model molmoact2-droid --task-id BananaInBowlTask --episodes 1
```

The CLI prints each stage, from `Getting a simulator` to `Running`, and a window
shows the simulation live. It ends with the task's score, such as
`BananaInBowlTask  1/1 (100%)`, and `Success rate: 1/1 (100%)`.

## Run a suite

```bash
dreamscale sim robolab --model molmoact2-droid --task-suite smoke10 --max-units 2
```

`smoke10` is ten single-object tasks; `all` is every RoboLab task. Without
`--task-id` or `--task-suite`, the run is `smoke10`.

`--model` takes any DROID model your organization can run: `molmoact2-droid`, `pi0.5-droid` and `praxis-1-droid`, and in beta `flux-3-action-droid`, `cosmos3-nano-policy-droid` and `cosmos3-edge-policy-droid`.

- `--episodes N` sets the episodes per task (default 4).
- `--max-units N` runs up to N tasks at once, 1 to 4.
- `--no-view` skips the live window.
- `--output-root DIR` saves each episode's video and result.
- Ctrl+C cancels the run.

`dreamscale sim robolab --list` lists the suites, and
`dreamscale sim robolab --task-suite smoke10 --list` lists a suite's tasks.

A run you start within 15 minutes of your last one reuses its simulator and
starts in seconds.

## Pricing

The simulator is free. The model is billed as on a robot, \$5 per robot-hour
while connected. A run is refused before it starts if your account has no
credits.
