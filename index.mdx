in real time. You don't need a local GPU or any model setup.

## How it works

- **Pick a model.** `dreamscale models` lists the models your organization can
  run. Each model is trained for a specific robot.
- **Start a session.** Dreamscale starts a GPU for you. The first connection
  can take a few minutes while the model loads.
- **Stream observations, get actions.** The CLI and SDK handle networking,
  buffering, and timing.
- **Pay per session.** Sessions bill per second from prepaid credits. Rates
  and your balance are on the [dashboard](https://app.dreamscalelabs.com/dashboard).

<Warning>
  Cloud inference does not replace your robot's safety controller. Keep joint
  and speed limits, a clear workspace, and a physical e-stop on every robot.
</Warning>
