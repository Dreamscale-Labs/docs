Each model expects observations in its own format and returns actions in its
own units. Build observations with the model's helper, and convert actions
back to your robot's units before sending them.

| Model | Robot | Helper | Actions per chunk × values | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `molmoact2-so101` | SO-101 | `dreamscale.so101.observe` | 30 × 6 | 30 Hz |
| `molmoact2-bimanual-yam` | Bimanual YAM | `dreamscale.yam.observe` | 30 × 14 | 30 Hz |
| `pi05-yam` | Bimanual YAM | `dreamscale.yam.observe` | 16 × 14 | 30 Hz |
| `pi05-trossen-widowx` | Trossen WidowX AI | `dreamscale.trossen.observe` | 50 × 14 | 30 Hz |
| `molmoact2-droid` | Franka (DROID) | `dreamscale.franka.observe` | 15 × 8 | 15 Hz |
| `molmoact2-libero` | LIBERO simulator | `dreamscale.libero.observe` | 10 × 7 | 10 Hz |
| Your deployment | Any | `dreamscale.robot.observe` | Set by the deployment | Set by the deployment |

Run `dreamscale models` for every model your organization can use. All camera
frames are H×W×3 `uint8` RGB.

<Warning>
  Matching the vector length is not enough. Joint order, units, camera order,
  and gripper convention must all match, or the robot moves to the wrong place.
</Warning>

## MolmoAct2 SO-101

```python
observation = dreamscale.so101.observe(
    side_frame=side_rgb,
    wrist_frame=wrist_rgb,
    joint_positions=[shoulder_pan, shoulder_lift, elbow_flex, wrist_flex, wrist_roll, gripper],
)
```

- Joints are in LeRobot SO-101 degrees. The gripper uses LeRobot's 0–100 range.
- Actions are absolute targets in the same order and units.

## Bimanual YAM

`molmoact2-bimanual-yam` and `pi05-yam` share one helper:

```python
observation = dreamscale.yam.observe(
    top_frame=top_rgb,
    left_frame=left_wrist_rgb,
    right_frame=right_wrist_rgb,
    left_joint_positions=left_six_joints,    # radians
    left_gripper=left_gripper,               # 0 closed to 1 open
    right_joint_positions=right_six_joints,  # radians
    right_gripper=right_gripper,             # 0 closed to 1 open
)
```

- Actions are absolute targets: left six joints, left gripper, right six joints,
  right gripper. Grippers are at indices 6 and 13.
- Grippers are continuous. Don't threshold them to open or closed.
- `pi05-yam` was trained on 640 × 360 frames.

## π0.5 Trossen WidowX

```python
observation = dreamscale.trossen.observe(
    left_wrist_frame=left_wrist_rgb,
    high_frame=high_rgb,
    right_wrist_frame=right_wrist_rgb,
    left_joint_positions=left_six_joints,    # radians
    left_gripper=left_carriage_position,     # robot's native units
    right_joint_positions=right_six_joints,  # radians
    right_gripper=right_carriage_position,
)
```

- Actions use the same order as YAM: left arm, left gripper, right arm, right
  gripper.
- Trained on one task: pouring items from a red cup. An empty instruction uses
  that task.

## MolmoAct2 DROID

```python
observation = dreamscale.franka.observe(
    exterior_frame=exterior_rgb,
    wrist_frame=wrist_rgb,
    joint_positions=seven_joints,  # radians
    gripper=gripper,               # 0 open to 1 closed
)
```

- Actions are seven absolute joint targets in radians, then the gripper.
- Pass `second_exterior_frame=` if you have a second exterior camera.

## MolmoAct2 LIBERO

```python
observation = dreamscale.libero.observe(
    agent_frame=agent_rgb,
    wrist_frame=wrist_rgb,
    state=[eef_x, eef_y, eef_z, axis_angle_x, axis_angle_y, axis_angle_z, gripper_qpos_0, gripper_qpos_1],
)
```

- State is end-effector position, end-effector rotation as an axis-angle
  vector, then the two gripper joint positions.
- Actions are robosuite delta actions:
  `[dx, dy, dz, d_axis_x, d_axis_y, d_axis_z, gripper]`. They only fit the
  LIBERO environment.

## Your deployment

A [deployment](/fine-tuning/deployments) names its own cameras and state width.
Key frames by the deployment's camera keys, in order, and send state in the
units your checkpoint was trained on:

```python
observation = dreamscale.robot.observe(
    cameras={"side_cam": side_rgb, "wrist_cam": wrist_rgb},
    state=joints_and_gripper,
)
```

The camera keys and state width are on the deployment's page in the dashboard.
