Each model runs one robot type: build its observations with that robot's helper. Frames
are H×W×3 `uint8` RGB at any size.

| Model | Robot | Chunk × values | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `molmoact2-so101` | SO-101 | 30 × 6 | 30 Hz |
| `flux-3-action-so101` | SO-101 | 42 × 6 | 30 Hz |
| `pi0.5-so101` | SO-101 | 30 × 6 | 30 Hz |
| `molmoact2-bimanual-yam` | Bimanual YAM | 30 × 14 | 30 Hz |
| `pi0.5-bimanualyam` | Bimanual YAM | 30 × 14 | 30 Hz |
| `molmoact2-droid` | [Franka](/robots/franka) | 15 × 8 | 15 Hz |
| `pi0.5-libero` | LIBERO simulator | 20 × 7 | 20 Hz |
| `gr00t-n1.7-libero` | LIBERO simulator | 16 × 7 | 20 Hz |
| `long-wam-libero` | LIBERO simulator | 32 × 7 | 20 Hz |
| `xiaomi-robotics-1-robocasa365` | RoboCasa365 simulator | 16 × 12 | 20 Hz |
| `long-wam-robocasa365` | RoboCasa365 simulator | 32 × 12 | 20 Hz |

Makers: Ai2 (MolmoAct2), Physical Intelligence (π0.5), Black Forest Labs (FLUX.3 Action),
Xiaomi (Xiaomi Robotics 1) and NVIDIA (GR00T N1.7, Long-WAM).

`dreamscale models list` shows which ones your organization can run.
The previous ids `pi05-libero`, `pi05-so101` and `pi05-yam` still work as aliases.

## SO-101

```python
observation = dreamscale.so101.observation(
    side_frame=side,
    wrist_frame=wrist,
    joint_positions=joints,  # 6 values
)
```

- Joint order: `shoulder_pan`, `shoulder_lift`, `elbow_flex`, `wrist_flex`,
  `wrist_roll`, `gripper`.
- Joints are in calibrated LeRobot degrees; the gripper is 0–100.
- Actions are absolute targets in the same order and units.
- `flux-3-action-so101` builds on the actions it already sent, so call it with
  `next_action`, not `predict`.

## Bimanual YAM

```python
observation = dreamscale.yam.observation(
    top_frame=top,
    left_frame=left_wrist,
    right_frame=right_wrist,
    left_joint_positions=left_joints,    # 6 values, radians
    left_gripper=left_gripper,           # 0 closed to 1 open
    right_joint_positions=right_joints,  # 6 values, radians
    right_gripper=right_gripper,         # 0 closed to 1 open
)
```

- Actions are 14 absolute targets: left joints 1–6, left gripper (index 6), right
  joints 1–6, right gripper (index 13).
- Grippers are continuous: don't threshold them.

## LIBERO

```python
observation = dreamscale.libero.observation(
    agent_frame=agent_view,  # agentview_image
    wrist_frame=wrist_view,  # robot0_eye_in_hand_image
    state=state,             # 8 values
)
```

- State: end-effector position (3), rotation as an axis-angle
  vector (3), then the two gripper joint positions.
- Pass frames as LIBERO renders them: the SDK flips them upright.
- Actions are LIBERO deltas: `dx`, `dy`, `dz`, three axis-angle rotation
  deltas, then the gripper (−1 open, +1 close).
- `long-wam-libero` runs in `dreamscale sim libero` only: this helper doesn't build its
  2.4 s camera window yet.
